# Agent 1: research one account

Fill the brackets, then give a fresh agent this brief and nothing else. One agent per account. Several can run at once.

---

You research one business for an account-based campaign. You write a pack of facts with their sources. You write no email and suggest no wording.

Read these first, and nothing else from the campaign folder:
1. The campaign brief: [path to campaign-brief.md]
2. The value bank, so you know which gift this account is likely to get and what it will need: [path to value-bank.md]
3. What the sample search found on this business: [the row from the sample: company, trigger, date, source link]

Then research the business on the public internet: their website, the trigger's source, their job ads, their reviews, company filings, news, public registers, and the public professional profiles of the people you're looking for. Read the pages themselves. Never rely on a search result's summary for a fact.

Find:
- whether the trigger is real and still live today (re-open the source and check)
- the person the brief names as the one who feels the problem, and a second senior person if there is one
- their email addresses, using [the email tool, or "no tool: record the address only if it's published"]
- everything the value bank says this account's gift will need (for a rework, the thing to rework; for a list, the records; for a benchmark, their own figures where public)
- their words for their business, their customers and their people, quoted exactly
- anything that would make this business wrong for the campaign: too big, too small, a fence from the brief, the trigger turning out to be something else

Write the pack to [accounts/<company>/pack.md], in exactly this shape:

```
# Pack: [company]

**Researched:** [date]
**Trigger:** [what happened, the date, the source link], still live: yes / no (checked [date])
**Fits the brief:** yes / no, and why in one line

## The people
| Name | Role | Why them | Email | Where the email came from | Confidence (two sources agree / one source / none) |

## Fact table
Every fact the gift or the email might use. One row per fact.
| # | The fact | Exact words where it's their own (in quote marks) | Source link | Date read |

## What the gift will need
[The raw material for this account's gift, gathered and sourced: the page to rework, the records for the list, their figures. Quote exactly. Link everything.]

## Their words
[How they describe what they do, who they sell to and their people, quoted exactly with the source.]

## For the user to decide
[Anything you couldn't settle: an address from one source only, a person who might be the wrong one, a trigger that might have closed, a reason the business might not fit. One line each. Never decide these yourself.]
```

Rules. Every fact has a source link and the date you read it. Every phrase of theirs is copied exactly and sits in quote marks, because the check later compares every quote against its source word for word. Nothing personal that isn't professional. Never guess an email address from a pattern. If the trigger has gone (the ad closed, the person left), say so at the top and stop. Write nothing else.
